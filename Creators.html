Meet the Creators!

The four Year 7 students who started it all — and are still building it.

Nathan

Original Idea & Story Writer

Favourite class: Mage

Hi, I’m Nathan and I am one of the main creators of Legend Of Fenrirak! I am the one who came up with the idea for Legend Of Fenrirak while everyone else was stumped at the start of our project! From then on all of us have been building on the idea to make the awesome board games! I then decided to write a story for Legend of Fenrirak, so far it has over 12,000 words in it! Also thank you for taking the time to view this simple website (created by me!), enjoy the rest of your day!

I bet Ronald in a game of The Thunder Dragon on the 25/08/2026 for the FIRST time, though he claims it was “unfair” due to a minor bug in the website 🙄. And yes, Mage IS the best class to play!

Justin

Co-Creator

Hello everyone, I’m Justin, one of the creators of Legend Of Fenrirak. Thank you for your interest in The Legend of Fenrirak™. We have poured our hearts and souls into creating this immersive gaming experience, and we truly hope you find it an exciting journey filled with adventure and challenges. As you embark on this epic quest, remember that your strategic prowess and ability to work as a team will be crucial in overcoming the formidable Fenrirak and achieving victory. Get ready to dive into a world teeming with monsters, where every decision you make will shape your destiny. Good luck, and may the best warriors emerge victorious.

Ashton

Character & Monster Artist

Hello, I am Ashton, I am one of the creators of Legend Of Fenrirak, I am the guy who drew most of the things in the first board game, like the characters and monsters. I think playing Legend Of Fenrirak is super cool because you get to hang out with your friends or family and do something different. It’s cool to see everyone get competitive and try to win, plus there’s always lots of laughing and joking around. Legend Of Fenrirak makes you think hard and plan your moves. It’s also a great way to take a break from phones and screens. Legend Of Fenrirak is just a blast and makes awesome memories!

Joy

Main Artist, The Ice Dragon

Hello, my name is Joy and I am the main artist for Legend Of Fenrirak: The Ice Dragon! I drew almost every character and enemy that appears in it. The coolest part is that everyone else who helped create The Ice Dragon was a Year 8, and I was only a Year 6 isn’t that amazing!? I think Legend Of Fenrirak is such a cool game, and I’m really proud of how the art turned out too!

Brendon

Member & Editor, The Ice Dragon

Greetings random person. I am Brendon, a late added member of The Legend Of Fenrirak, The Ice Dragon. Though I cannot think of much to write here, I do have something to say. Whilst in production of the board game, I was quite amazed by how structured and almost flawless the planning document was. The not-so-quite-attention to details was truly spectacular. Although I did get slightly controversial about the credibility of names used for skills and enemies, the final products turned out still to be somewhat satisfactory.
