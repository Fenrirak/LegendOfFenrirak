Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 24, 2026

Legend of Fenrirak is an independent, non-commercial hobby project. We collect as little data as possible, and this page explains exactly what that is.

The online games (Thunder Dragon & Ice Dragon)

These multiplayer games use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to tell players in the same room apart. This creates a random, temporary ID for your browser session — it is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information, and we cannot use it to identify you.

While you're in a game room, the following is stored temporarily so the game can sync between players:

  • The display name you choose to enter (up to 16 characters)
  • In-game state: health, coins, energy, inventory, and similar game values
  • The room code, which is only ever shared by you to people you invite

Room data isn't publicly listable or searchable — it can only be read by someone who already has the room code. Rooms are deleted once a game finishes or is closed.

Showdowns

Showdowns is a single-player game that runs entirely in your own browser. No account, server, or game data is created or stored by us.

Feedback form

After a game of Showdowns, we optionally invite you to fill in a short Google Form. Doing so is entirely your choice. Responses are collected and stored by Google Forms, and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Support the Creators / Buy Me a Coffee

The "Support" button on this site links out to Buy Me a Coffee, a separate third-party website. We don't see or handle any payment information — that's processed entirely by Buy Me a Coffee under their own privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising of any kind. The only cookie-like data used is Firebase's own anonymous authentication token, described above.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log standard technical information (such as IP addresses) as part of serving the site, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Children's privacy

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the data described above is not personal information in the first place. If you're a parent or guardian with a concern, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
